# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want UniDock to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their background, experience, country, gender, religion, or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that break this code, and may ban contributors who repeatedly do so.

Report problems privately to the maintainers through a [GitHub security advisory](../../security/advisories/new) or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are kept confidential.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
